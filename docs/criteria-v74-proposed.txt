Spec: NY Suburbs Home Evaluator

Goal
Score one listing against a fixed buyer profile. Output: Reject or score (0–100), verdict, key warnings, confidence.

Hard Requirements (any fail = Reject, score 0)
Location must be in New York State (NJ listings are a hard pass — Reject immediately)
Minimum 2,200 sqft
Minimum 3 bedrooms
If only 3 bedrooms, must also have a separate office / den / flex room that can function as a real office
Price between $850,000 and $2,250,000 (the target range is $1.5M-$2M; above $2M is workable but less desirable; $2.25M is a hard cap)
Detached single-family only
Hiking nearby and not dense urban
On the market, pending, or pre-listing (do NOT hard-reject for unknown status)
Note: Brokers often share pre-listings before they go live. Treat null listing_status as unknown, not a fail.
Only hard-reject if the property is EXPLICITLY confirmed sold (e.g. listing_status = 'Sold', sale price confirmed, or description unambiguously describes a completed sale with no re-listing signal).

Commute of 110 minutes or more door-to-door to Brookfield Place = Reject (hard fail). If commute is unknown, mark unknown — do not reject.

Base score: 30

Commute is #3 overall — penalize heavily beyond 90 min.

Space & Neighbor Separation (important factor — judge it on EVERY listing)
The buyer wants meaningful separation from neighbors — not countryside, but not
dense either. Space around the house with a woodsy feel is a strong positive;
visibly close neighbors are a reason the buyer passes on otherwise-good homes.
The reference bar: Armonk NY suburban lots where houses are clearly detached
with visible greenery, yard, or distance between them.

Anchor the judgment to lot_acres FIRST, then confirm or adjust with photos:
  - lot under 0.35 acres: assume tight (-5 to -8). NEVER award separation bonus
    points on a lot under 0.4 acres — mature landscaping or a pretty yard photo
    is not separation; the neighbors are still right there.
  - 0.35-0.7 acres: neutral by default; use exterior/aerial photos to move
    up (+3 to +5 visible side buffer) or down (-5 neighbors visibly close).
  - over 0.7 acres, or photos showing woods/greenery buffer between houses:
    +8 to +10 (the woodsy, private feel this buyer wants).

When examining exterior and aerial photos, look specifically for neighboring
HOUSES visible near the property line — visible close neighbors means tight
(-5 to -8) even on a mid-size lot.
  -10  very tight — row-house style, shared walls, or attached structures

If no exterior images, aerial view, or lot size available: apply 0 (do not
penalize for missing data).
Note: this is NOT a hard requirement. Dense does not Reject. It is a meaningful soft factor.

Age & Physical Condition (use age_condition.age_tier and age_condition.keywords_matched from structured data)
Use the pre-computed age_condition signal when available.

Age penalties reflect unrenovated condition risk, not age alone. A fully gut-renovated older home should net close to 0 — good bones and classic layouts are a positive. Penalize condition, not calendar.

Age baseline (before renovation offsets):
  0   2005+ (new construction — no penalty)
  -1  1990-2004 (minor age penalty)
  -3  1975-1989
  -5  1960-1974
  -8  1940-1959
  -10 pre-1940

Renovation offsets (can fully offset age penalty for well-renovated older homes):
  +8 to +10  gut renovated / fully renovated (kitchen, bathrooms, systems — the whole house)
  +4 to +6   meaningful updates (updated kitchen or baths, new roof, new HVAC, new windows)
  0           cosmetic updates only
  -12 to -15  sold as-is or fixer-upper (keywords: as is, fixer, needs tlc)
  -6          cesspool or oil heat present (mechanical liability)
  -15 to -20  major structural/mechanical renovation required

Square Footage Provenance (use sqft_provenance from structured data when available)
MLS listings state where the square footage came from. Only a municipal
measurement (assessor, tax roll, public record) is third-party verified; every
other source is self-reported by the owner, builder or agent.
  sqft_provenance.verified = true — the number is municipal; trust it as stated.
  sqft_provenance.verified = false — the size is SELF-REPORTED. Add a concern:
    "Square footage is <source>-reported — needs manual verification." Do NOT
    reject and do NOT deduct points for the provenance itself. Instead lower
    confidence one step and treat every sqft-derived signal (price per sqft, the
    2,200 sqft minimum, the 2,200-2,400 borderline penalty, the oversized-home
    penalties) as approximate. A listing must never fail the 2,200 sqft hard
    requirement on an unverified number alone — when the stated size is within
    10% of the minimum, mark that requirement unknown rather than failed.
  sqft_provenance.verified = null, or the field is absent — source unknown. No
    concern and no adjustment: most listings simply do not publish the field,
    and flagging all of them would make the flag meaningless.

Price per Sqft vs Market (use price_per_sqft_signal from structured data when available)
  +10   below_market (15%+ below Zillow median benchmark for the ZIP)
  0     at_market
  -10   above_market (20%+ above Zillow median benchmark for the ZIP)
  If no Zillow data: omit this adjustment entirely

Commute to Brookfield Place (twice-weekly; 110+ min = hard reject)
Buyer commutes twice per week. Long commutes weigh heavily — tolerance drops
sharply after 75 minutes, and anything approaching the 110 limit should score
poorly even when the house itself is great. Score this curve on the reported
door-to-door number; the station-drive penalty below stacks on top of it.
+15 65 min or less door-to-door
+8  65-75 min
-5  75-85 min
-12 85-90 min
-20 90-95 min
-28 95-100 min
-38 100-109 min
REJECT at 110 min or more (hard fail — too far)
The hard reject uses the reported door-to-door number, never the
parking-adjusted one described below.

Station Access — DRIVE TIME AND PARKING (use nearest_metro_north and
commute_data from structured data when available)
The buyer DRIVES to the station. WALKING distance to the station is irrelevant:
it must NEVER cost points and must NEVER be listed as a concern — do not
penalize a home for being unwalkable to the platform.
What does matter is the DRIVE to the station plus the time it takes to park
there. The door-to-door number covers the drive but NOT the parking, which in
practice runs about 10 minutes each way. So state the real burden in
property_summary as the reported number plus that parking buffer, and apply
this penalty on top of the curve above:
  +3  station drive 5 minutes or less (or a genuinely walkable station)
  0   6-8 minute station drive (the normal, expected case)
  -4  9-12 minute station drive
  -8  13-17 minute station drive
  -15 18 minutes or more — a long drive plus a parking hunt, twice a day, is a
      real recurring cost and on its own a reason the buyer passes
Read the station drive from commute_data.drive_minutes when present. Otherwise
estimate it from nearest_metro_north.distance_m (roughly 1 driving minute per
800m) and say in property_summary that the figure is an estimate.
If no station data of any kind is available: no adjustment.

Rail Corridor / Location Preference (SOFT — buyer prefers the Hudson/Harlem side)
The buyer prefers the Hudson River side and central corridor (Briarcliff Manor
area) over the eastern New Haven Line toward Stamford/CT. This is a preference,
NOT a dealbreaker — an exceptional home in a dispreferred town can still earn
Worth Touring on its merits.
  PREFERRED (no penalty) — Hudson Line + Harlem Line towns, e.g. Briarcliff Manor,
    Ossining, Croton-on-Hudson, Cortlandt, Dobbs Ferry, Irvington, Tarrytown,
    Sleepy Hollow, Pleasantville, Chappaqua, Mount Kisco, Bedford, Katonah,
    Somers, Yorktown Heights, Thornwood, Hawthorne, Valhalla, Mount Pleasant.
  -15 DISPREFERRED (soft penalty) — the New Haven Line corridor toward New
    Rochelle and Stamford/CT: New Rochelle, Larchmont, Mamaroneck, Harrison,
    Rye, Rye Brook, Port Chester. Apply a real but SURMOUNTABLE -15 penalty and
    note the location as a concern. Do NOT reject and do NOT force to Low
    Priority — a strong home here (great schools, space, price, short commute)
    can still reach Worth Touring. The buyer passed on New Rochelle before, but
    in theory the right house there could make sense.
  NOT dispreferred (do NOT penalize): Purchase and West Harrison are fine —
    they sit inland toward White Plains, not the CT corridor. Everywhere not
    explicitly in the dispreferred list above is acceptable.
  If the town is ambiguous or not listed, do not penalize.

Lot & Drainage (use lot_acres structured field when available)

Power Line Proximity (use power_line_proximity from structured data when available)
High-voltage transmission lines (power=line or power=cable in OSM) near a property are a meaningful concern — health perception, noise, visual impact. Distribution poles (power=pole) are not flagged.
Apply based on nearest_distance_m field:
  0   300m or more (no concern)
  -5  150–299m (moderate proximity — note as concern)
  -10 75–149m (close proximity — flag as significant concern)
  -15 under 75m (very close — flag as serious concern; deduct heavily)
If power_line_proximity data is absent or nearest_distance_m is null: no deduction (do not penalize for missing data).
Include count_within_300m and voltage in the concerns section if deducting points.

Flood Zone (use flood_zone from structured data when available)
FEMA flood zone designation from the National Flood Hazard Layer (NFHL).
SFHA zones (A, AE, AH, AO, AR, A99, V, VE) require mandatory flood insurance and carry real risk.
  -5 to -8  Zone AE, VE, or other SFHA where property is marketed as waterfront/lakefront (flood zone is expected and priced in; flood insurance typically $800-1,500/yr)
  -10 to -12 Zone AE, VE, or other SFHA for a non-waterfront property (unexpected flood risk — flag as significant concern)
  -8  Zone A (SFHA, no base flood elevation determined)
  0   Zone X 500-year (0.2% annual chance; acceptable risk, no deduction)
  0   Zone X minimal hazard (standard expectation; no deduction)
If flood_zone data is absent: no deduction (do not penalize for missing data).
Include fld_zone and zone_subty in the concerns section if deducting points.


HOA (use hoa_monthly from structured data when available)
Do not penalize HOA fees directly. Treat HOA as part of effective carrying cost:
note the monthly fee in concerns and fold it into overall affordability context.
Flag if HOA exceeds $500/month as a mild cost concern.
If hoa_monthly = 0: explicitly note no HOA (positive signal for carrying cost).
If hoa_monthly data is absent: no assumption — infer from description if possible.

Handling Unknowns for Layout-Dependent Requirements
- Verifiable unknown (floor plans present but feature unconfirmed): deduct 10-15 pts, flag as high risk.
- Missing data unknown (no floor plans or insufficient images): deduct 3-5 pts only, flag as pending verification.
For non-layout requirements (detached structure, hiking proximity), unknowns warrant a moderate 5-8 pt deduction if no exterior shots or context available.
